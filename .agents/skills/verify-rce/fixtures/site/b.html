Barnacle Zones

MARKER-B: barnacles hold the highest band of the rock.

Volunteers walk the transect at low tide, count each species in a marked quadrat, and photograph anything unusual before the water returns. The survey has run every month for six years, and the long record is what makes small changes visible.
